# Kisspeptin-10

## Summary

C-terminal decapeptide of kisspeptin (metastin 45–54), the neuropeptide that gates GnRH release and therefore puberty and reproduction. Investigational: studied as a physiological stimulator of gonadotropins and as a potential therapeutic; no approved product.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** kisspeptin-receptor (KISS1R) agonism on GnRH neurons, releasing GnRH and hence LH/FSH in a dose- and sex-dependent manner.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications.** Study doses are research conditions only.

## Important safety information and cautions

Investigational; chronic administration down-regulates responses in some settings (tachyphylaxis); long-term effects unknown.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: pregnancy, hormone-sensitive cancers (mechanism-inferred).

## Side effects

Trial data: generally well tolerated; injection-site reactions.

## Product quality

Unregulated supply; quality unknown.

Document source and dose; gonadotropin effects may interact with fertility investigations; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** The 'switch' peptide of reproduction, under study as a medicine. **What do we know?** It reliably raises reproductive hormones in trials. **What don't we know?** Long-term therapeutic value. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.
