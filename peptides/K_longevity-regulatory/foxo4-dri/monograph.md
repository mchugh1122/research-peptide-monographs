# FOXO4-DRI

## Summary

D-retro-inverso peptide that disrupts FOXO4–p53 interaction, causing selective apoptosis of senescent cells ('senolytic'). The 2017 Cell paper showed rejuvenating effects in mice. Preclinical only; sold as a 'research' anti-ageing peptide.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** blockade of the FOXO4-p53 interaction releases p53-mediated apoptosis specifically in senescent cells, improving function in aged and chemotherapy-treated mice.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Animal doses are study conditions.

## Important safety information and cautions

No human data; senolytics carry theoretical risks (off-target apoptosis, unknown long-term effects); D-peptide immunogenicity profile unstudied in humans.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: pregnancy, active malignancy (paradoxical effects conceivable).

## Side effects

Unknown in humans.
