# Epitalon

## Summary

Tetrapeptide Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly synthesised by the Khavinson group, claimed to regulate pineal function, melatonin and telomere biology. Mainly Russian literature; the 'telomerase activation' claims lack independent replication. Reviewed by the FDA PCAC in July 2026, which narrowly recommended it for the 503A bulks list (insomnia).

## Mechanism of action

**Proposed:** modulation of pineal/melatonin systems and gene expression; telomerase-activation claims are reported in Russian studies but not independently confirmed.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A four-amino-acid 'longevity' peptide from Russia. **What do we know?** Claims rest mainly on one group's studies. **What don't we know?** Whether the telomere claims are real — they are not independently confirmed. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No Western randomised trials
