# Dihexa

## Summary

N-hexanoic-Tyr-Ile-(6-aminohexanoic) amide: a small pseudo-peptide derived from angiotensin IV, not a true peptide. Preclinical studies report procognitive and synaptogenic activity via the HGF/c-Met pathway. No human trials; sold as a 'research' nootropic.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** activation of hepatocyte growth factor (HGF)/c-Met signalling, promoting synapse formation in vitro and in rodent models. Human data absent.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Animal doses are study conditions.

## Important safety information and cautions

No human safety data of any kind. HGF/c-Met agonism is a cancer-relevant pathway (mechanism-inferred precaution). Unregulated supply risks apply.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: active or prior malignancy, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Unknown in humans.
