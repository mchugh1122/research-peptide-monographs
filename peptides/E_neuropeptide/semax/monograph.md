# Semax

## Summary

Heptapeptide (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro), an analogue of ACTH(4-10) modified with a Pro-Gly-Pro tail. Registered in Russia as a nootropic/neuroprotective agent (verify); unapproved in the US and EU. Reviewed by the FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee in July 2026, which narrowly recommended it for the 503A bulks list; final FDA action is pending.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** modulation of dopaminergic and serotonergic transmission; neuroprotective effects in ischaemia models. Human mechanisms are extrapolated.

## Indications and dose

**No US/EU licence. No established dose.** Russian registration covers neurological indications (verify against Russian regulator records); doses are per the Russian label.

## Important safety information and cautions

Clinical evidence is largely Russian-language and pre-dates modern trial standards; the FDA PCAC evaluation raised characterisation concerns common to unapproved peptides. Injectable immunogenicity risk applies.

## Contraindications

Per Russian label (verify); not transcribed here.

## Side effects

Local nasal irritation reported with the nasal form; systematic data lacking in English.

## Product quality

Unapproved outside Russia; 'research' supply is unregulated.

## Handling and storage

No guidance for unapproved supply.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A Russian nootropic peptide based on a stress-hormone fragment. **What do we know?** Animal data and Russian clinical experience; limited modern trial evidence. **What don't we know?** Efficacy by modern standards outside Russia. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it, the route, and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No modern randomised trials in English-language literature
- Pharmacokinetics in humans poorly documented
- Independent analyses of non-Russian market supply pending
