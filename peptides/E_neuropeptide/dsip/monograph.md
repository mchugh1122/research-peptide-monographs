# DSIP (emideltide)

## Summary

Delta sleep-inducing peptide (Trp-Ala-Gly-Gly-Asp-Ala-Ser-Gly-Glu), a nonapeptide first isolated in 1977; emideltide is the same peptide under a different name. The original sleep-promoting claims were never convincingly replicated in humans. Reviewed by the FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee in July 2026, which voted against 503A inclusion — the only peptide of seven to be rejected.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** none. **Proposed:** sleep modulation via opioid and neuroendocrine interactions; the 2006 review concludes the peptide remains mechanistically unresolved.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Proposed compounding uses (insomnia, narcolepsy, opioid withdrawal) were rejected by the PCAC; FDA-approved therapies exist for all three.

## Important safety information and cautions

FDA: not well characterized (impurities, aggregates, endotoxin data lacking); immunogenicity risk for injection; no nonclinical toxicity studies; possible opioid-system interactions (addiction liability unstudied). Older IV studies reported hypotension in a few subjects.

## Contraindications

None established.

## Side effects

Older IV data: perspiration, headache, nausea, vertigo; hypotension in rare cases.

## Product quality

FDA concluded the substance is not well characterized; market supply is unregulated.

## Handling and storage

Instructions deliberately not given.

## Notes for clinicians

Document source and dose; note the PCAC's conclusion that evidence for all proposed uses is inadequate; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A 1970s 'sleep peptide' whose claims never held up. **What do we know?** It exists; human sleep benefits were never reliably shown. **What don't we know?** Nearly everything about safety and effect — an FDA committee voted against it in 2026. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No modern controlled efficacy trials
- No nonclinical toxicity programme
- Independent analyses of market supply pending
