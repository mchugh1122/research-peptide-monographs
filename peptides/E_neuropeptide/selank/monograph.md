# Selank

## Summary

Heptapeptide (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro) derived from the immunomodulatory peptide tuftsin, with a Pro-Gly-Pro tail shared with Semax. Registered in Russia as an anxiolytic (verify); unapproved in the US and EU. Sold as a 'research' anxiolytic.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** anxiolytic activity, partly via modulation of GABAergic transmission and enkephalin metabolism; details reviewed in molecular studies. Human mechanisms are extrapolated.
