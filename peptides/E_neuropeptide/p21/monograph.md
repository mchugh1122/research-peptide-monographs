# P21

## Summary

Acetylated peptide derived from ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF), studied for cognitive rescue in animal models including Down-syndrome mice. Preclinical only; no human trials. Sequence to verify.

## Mechanism of action

**Proposed (animal):** neurotrophic signalling promoting hippocampal neurogenesis, synaptogenesis and memory in rodent models.
