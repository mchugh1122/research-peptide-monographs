# GHRP-6

## Summary

Growth-hormone-releasing peptide 6 (His-D-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2), the original synthetic GHRP. Investigated as a diagnostic and therapeutic growth-hormone secretagogue; development as a medicine was not completed. Sold as a 'research' peptide, alone and in combinations.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** ghrelin-receptor agonism releasing growth hormone; appetite stimulation (ghrelin-like). Animal studies also suggest tissue-protective effects in injury models (to be graded).

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Historical trial doses were study conditions.

## Important safety information and cautions

GH-axis and appetite effects; no long-term human safety database. Unregulated supply adds identity risks.

## Contraindications

None formally established. Precautionary: active malignancy, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Hunger, flushing, headache; hypoglycaemia risk is low but present with prolonged fasting contexts (anecdotal).

## Product quality

Unregulated supply; quality unknown.
