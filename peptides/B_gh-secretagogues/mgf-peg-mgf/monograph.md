# MGF and PEG-MGF

## Summary

Mechano growth factor (MGF) is a C-terminal splice-variant peptide of IGF-1 (IGF-1Ec) implicated in muscle repair after mechanical load; the sequence shown is the 24-residue E-peptide with a C-terminal amide. PEG-MGF is a polyethylene-glycol-conjugated form claimed to have longer half-life. Neither has an approved product.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** none clinically. **Proposed (animal):** activation of muscle satellite (stem) cells and local repair signalling after damage or loading.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.**

## Important safety information and cautions

No human safety data identified. The PEGylated form's pharmacokinetics in humans are unpublished.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: active malignancy (IGF-axis peptide), pregnancy.

## Side effects

Unknown.
