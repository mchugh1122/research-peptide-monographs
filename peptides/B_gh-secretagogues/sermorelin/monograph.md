# Sermorelin

## Summary

GHRH(1–29) amide, the bioactive N-terminal fragment of growth-hormone-releasing hormone. Formerly marketed (Geref, and as a diagnostic). The branded product was discontinued in the US; sermorelin is now mainly compounded and used off-label for alleged anti-ageing or body-composition effects.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** GHRH-receptor agonism, stimulating pituitary growth-hormone secretion with a physiological pulse pattern. Short half-life (minutes) limits practicality; typically given daily by injection.

## Indications and dose

**No currently marketed licensed product in the US.** Historical uses: diagnosis and treatment of growth-hormone deficiency in children; dosing followed the former label. Compounded doses vary; none are endorsed here.

## Important safety information and cautions

Growth-hormone-axis stimulation can raise IGF-1; theoretical risks mirror GH therapies (fluid retention, glucose effects). Compounded products are not FDA-approved and quality varies.

## Contraindications

Per the former label: pregnancy, active malignancy, disruption of the pituitary axis. Verify.

## Side effects

Injection-site reactions, flushing, headache; data are historical.

## Product quality

Compounded supply is the norm; sterility and identity depend entirely on the compounding facility.

## Handling and storage

Follow the compounding pharmacy's instructions; no validated public data entered.

## Notes for clinicians

Ask about source and dose; check IGF-1 if GH-axis effects are suspected; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A discontinued growth-hormone-stimulating injection now sold through compounding pharmacies. **What do we know?** It worked for GH deficiency testing/treatment historically. **What don't we know?** Whether off-label anti-ageing claims have any basis. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No modern controlled trials for off-label uses
- Compounded product quality uncharacterized
