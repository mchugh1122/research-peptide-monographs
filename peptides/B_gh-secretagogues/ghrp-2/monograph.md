# GHRP-2

## Summary

Growth-hormone-releasing peptide 2 (pralmorelin; D-Ala-D-2Nal-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2), a synthetic ghrelin-receptor agonist. Approved in Japan as a diagnostic for growth-hormone deficiency; not approved elsewhere as a medicine. Widely sold as a 'research' peptide.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** ghrelin-receptor agonism releasing growth hormone from the pituitary; also stimulates appetite (ghrelin-like).

## Indications and dose

**Licensed (Japan):** diagnostic testing of growth-hormone secretion. Doses per the Japanese label; verify. No therapeutic indication elsewhere.

## Important safety information and cautions

Appetite stimulation and GH-axis effects; limited long-term data. Unregulated supply elsewhere adds quality risks.

## Contraindications

Per Japanese label; verify. Precautionary: active malignancy, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Flushing, hunger, headache; per diagnostic-use experience.

## Product quality

Approved product exists only in Japan; supply elsewhere is unregulated.

## Handling and storage

Follow the approved label where authorised; no advice for unapproved supply.

## Notes for clinicians

Document source, dose and appetite symptoms; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A peptide that releases growth hormone and stimulates appetite. **What do we know?** It is a diagnostic medicine in Japan. **What don't we know?** Safety of chronic self-use. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.
