# Tesamorelin

## Summary

Growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analogue: GHRH(1–44) with a trans-3-hexenoyl group at the N-terminus. Approved in the US (2010) for reduction of excess abdominal (visceral) fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. It is not approved for general weight loss, bodybuilding or anti-ageing uses.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** GHRH-receptor agonism at the pituitary, stimulating endogenous growth-hormone secretion (physiological pattern) and downstream IGF-1. The hexenoyl modification extends half-life versus native GHRH.

## Indications and dose

**Licensed indication (US):** reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected patients with lipodystrophy; 2 mg subcutaneous once daily. Verify against the current label.

## Important safety information and cautions

Warnings include fluid retention, hypersensitivity reactions, and effects of elevated IGF-1 (arthralgia, carpal tunnel, glucose intolerance). Not studied for weight loss in people without HIV lipodystrophy.

## Contraindications

Pregnancy; disruption of the hypothalamic-pituitary axis (pituitary surgery, irradiation, tumour); active malignancy; hypersensitivity. Per label; verify.

## Side effects

Injection-site reactions (bruising, erythema), arthralgia, peripheral oedema, glucose elevations.

## Product quality

Approved product exists. Off-label and compounded supply is a separate, unregulated question; quality unknown.

## Handling and storage

Follow the approved product label. No advice is given for unapproved supplies.

## Notes for clinicians

Off-label use for 'anti-ageing' or performance is common; discuss evidence gaps, monitor IGF-1, glucose and fluid status, and report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A daily injection that makes the pituitary release growth hormone; approved for one narrow use (belly fat in HIV lipodystrophy). **What do we know?** It reduces visceral fat in that population. **What don't we know?** Long-term safety of off-label uses. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and why.

## Evidence gaps

- No trials of general weight loss or performance claims
- Long-term safety data limited
