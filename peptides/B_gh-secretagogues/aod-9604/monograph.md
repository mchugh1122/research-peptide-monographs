# AOD-9604

## Summary

Synthetic fragment of human growth hormone, hGH(177–191), with an added N-terminal tyrosine; the recorded structure includes a Cys6–Cys14 disulfide. Investigated as an anti-obesity agent; development was not completed and no product is approved. The registry note that FDA has described serious adverse events possibly associated with it is to be verified against the FDA documents.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** none clinically. **Proposed:** a fragment claimed to retain the lipolytic (fat-mobilising) effects of growth hormone without the growth effects; the claim rests on limited data, and clinical development was discontinued.
