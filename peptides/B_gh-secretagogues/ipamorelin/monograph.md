# Ipamorelin

## Summary

Pentapeptide growth-hormone secretagogue (Aib-His-D-2Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) acting at the ghrelin (GHS) receptor. Developed as a selective GH-releasing peptide; no approved product exists. Sold as a 'research' peptide, often in combinations.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** ghrelin-receptor agonism stimulating pituitary growth-hormone release; described as more selective (less ACTH/prolactin release) than earlier GHRPs in animal work. Human data are limited.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Published doses are study conditions only.

## Important safety information and cautions

No approved-product safety database. Class effects of GH secretagogues (fluid retention, glucose effects, IGF-1 elevation) are expected; unregulated supply adds identity risks.

## Contraindications

None formally established. Precautionary: active malignancy, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Limited human data; injection-site reactions reported anecdotally. To be updated from primary sources.

## Product quality

Unregulated supply; quality unknown. Independent analyses to be entered.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A lab-made peptide that triggers growth-hormone release. **What do we know?** Animal studies and limited human work; no approved medicine. **What don't we know?** Long-term safety. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.
