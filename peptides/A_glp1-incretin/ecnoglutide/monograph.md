# Ecnoglutide

## Summary

Long-acting, once-weekly GLP-1 receptor agonist described as the first cAMP-biased GLP-1 agonist; composed only of natural amino acids with a fatty-acid conjugation for half-life extension (XW003). Approved in China for type 2 diabetes and weight management; not approved in the US or EU.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** GLP-1 receptor agonism biased towards cAMP signalling over receptor internalisation in vitro. Whether biased agonism confers a clinical advantage over balanced GLP-1 agonists is debated (an accompanying editorial questioned it); clinical outcomes define the profile.

## Indications and dose

**Licensed indications (China):** type 2 diabetes and weight management; phase 3 doses 1.2–2.4 mg once weekly. Verify against the NMPA-approved label.

## Important safety information and cautions

Gastrointestinal events (nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea) most common, mostly mild–moderate. Class considerations (thyroid C-cell tumours in rodents, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease) presumed to apply until the label states otherwise.

## Contraindications

Per approved label (China); to be transcribed from the official label. Pregnancy safety data lacking.

## Side effects

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, decreased appetite.

## Product quality

Approved product exists in China. Supply elsewhere is unregulated unless lawfully imported; quality unknown.

## Handling and storage

Follow the approved label in jurisdictions where authorised.

## Notes for clinicians

Grey-market supply outside China should be documented as unknown product. Monitor GI tolerability and glycaemia; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A weekly GLP-1 injection approved in China. **What do we know?** Phase 3 trials show strong weight loss and glucose control. **What don’t we know?** Long-term safety; whether the ‘biased’ mechanism matters clinically. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- Data outside Chinese populations limited
- Cardiovascular outcomes unknown
- Independent analysis of unregulated supply
