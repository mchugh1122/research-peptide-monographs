# Mazdutide

## Summary

Once-weekly dual GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist derived from oxyntomodulin (IBI362/LY3305677) with a fatty-acid side chain. Developed and approved in China for chronic weight management and type 2 diabetes; not approved in the US or EU. Chinese approvals make it one of the few glucagon-pathway peptides with marketing authorisation anywhere.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** GLP-1 receptor agonism (appetite, glucose) plus glucagon-receptor agonism proposed to increase energy expenditure and hepatic fat oxidation. Weight loss in trials is dose-dependent and greater than GLP-1 monoagonism at comparable dose levels.

## Evidence summary

| Tier | Finding | Ref. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Phase 2: −11.3% body weight at 24 weeks (6 mg) | [10.1038/s41467-023-44067-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44067-4) |
| 2 | Phase 1b: −9.5% at 16 weeks (10 mg cohort) | [10.1016/j.eclinm.2022.101691](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2022.101691) |

## Indications and dose

**Licensed indications (China):** chronic weight management (BMI ≥28, or ≥24 with a comorbidity) and glycaemic control in type 2 diabetes; 9 mg dose evaluated in phase 3 (GLORY). Verify against the NMPA-approved label.

## Important safety information and cautions

Gastrointestinal adverse events (nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting) are the dominant tolerability issue. Long-term and cardiovascular safety data are limited relative to GLP-1 monoagonists.

## Contraindications

Per approved label (China); to be transcribed from the official label. Pregnancy safety data lacking.

## Side effects

Nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting; upper respiratory tract infection reported in phase 2.

## Product quality

Approved product exists in China. Any supply elsewhere is unregulated unless imported lawfully; quality unknown.

## Handling and storage

Follow the approved label in jurisdictions where authorised.

## Notes for clinicians

Be aware of grey-market supply outside China. Document source and dose; GI tolerability and glycaemia are the practical monitoring points; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A weekly injection approved in China for weight and glucose control. **What do we know?** Trials show strong dose-dependent weight loss. **What don’t we know?** Long-term safety outside Chinese populations. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.
