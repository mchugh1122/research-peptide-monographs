# PNC-27

## Summary

Chimeric peptide combining a p53-derived sequence with a membrane-translocation (penetratin) domain, designed to kill cancer cells via membrane-associated HDM-2. Preclinical in vitro/animal work only; no human trials. Sequence to verify.

## Mechanism of action

**Proposed (in vitro/animal):** binding of membrane-bound HDM-2 induces pore formation and rapid cancer-cell necrosis.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Preclinical only.

## Important safety information and cautions

No human data; membrane-active cytolytic peptides have unpredictable systemic toxicity. Unregulated supply is a real hazard.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: pregnancy; any non-cancer use is unjustified.

## Side effects
