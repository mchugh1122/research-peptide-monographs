# ARA-290

## Summary

Cibinetide: an 11-residue peptide derived from the helix-B surface of erythropoietin (N-terminal pyroglutamate), designed to activate the innate repair receptor (EPOR/βcR) without erythropoiesis. Investigational for small fibre neuropathy in sarcoidosis; not approved.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** agonism of the tissue-protective EPOR–β-common-receptor complex; reduces neuropathic inflammation without raising red-cell mass.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications.** Trial doses are study conditions.

## Important safety information and cautions

Erythropoietin-family signalling carries thrombosis theoretical considerations; trial safety data limited; unregulated supply risks apply.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: malignancy, polycythaemia (mechanism-inferred).

## Side effects

Trial data: generally well tolerated; injection-site reactions.

## Product quality

Unregulated supply; quality unknown.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A designer fragment of erythropoietin that treats nerve pain without making blood. **What do we know?** Promising early nerve-healing signals. **What don't we know?** Long-term safety and phase 3 results. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.
