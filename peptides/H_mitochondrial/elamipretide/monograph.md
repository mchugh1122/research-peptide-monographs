# Elamipretide (SS-31)

## Summary

Tetrapeptide (D-Arg-Dmt-Lys-Phe-NH2) targeting cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane, stabilising mitochondrial function. Received US accelerated approval (September 2025) for Barth syndrome, a rare cardioskeletal myopathy. Phase 3 in primary mitochondrial myopathy did not meet its primary endpoint.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** cardiolipin binding and mitochondrial membrane stabilisation, reducing oxidative stress and improving energy metabolism in affected tissues.

## Indications and dose

**Licensed (US):** Barth syndrome (accelerated approval, 2025; verify label and dosing). Confirmatory trial requirements apply to accelerated approvals.

## Important safety information and cautions

Infusion-site reactions; no major systemic signals in trials to date; accelerated approval means benefit confirmation is pending.

## Contraindications

Per label (verify).

## Side effects

Injection/infusion-site reactions, headache, fatigue.

## Product quality

## Notes for clinicians

Barth syndrome care is specialist-led; confirmatory-trial status matters for counselling; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A mitochondria-stabilising injection approved for a rare heart-muscle disease. **What do we know?** It helps in Barth syndrome; a larger trial in another mitochondrial disease was negative. **What don't we know?** Long-term confirmatory results. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- Confirmatory trial for accelerated approval pending
- Benefit in primary mitochondrial myopathy not shown
