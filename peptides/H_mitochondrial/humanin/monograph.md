# Humanin

## Summary

24-residue mitochondrial-derived peptide first identified as a neuroprotective factor against Alzheimer's-disease-associated neuronal death. Preclinical neuroprotection and metabolic data; no human therapeutic trials. Sold as a 'research' longevity peptide.

## Mechanism of action

**Proposed (animal):** cytoprotection via extracellular receptors and intracellular interactions, reducing apoptosis; precise mechanisms partially characterised.
