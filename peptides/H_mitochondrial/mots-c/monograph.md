# MOTS-c

## Summary

16-residue peptide encoded in mitochondrial 12S rRNA, proposed as a mitochondrial-derived metabolic signal. Animal data show metabolic benefits; human data are observational and small interventional studies. No approved product; reviewed (and recommended) by the FDA PCAC in July 2026 for the 503A bulks list.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** modulation of folate/AMPK pathways and metabolic regulation, including insulin sensitivity and exercise-related signalling. Human mechanisms inferred.
