# LL-37

## Summary

Human cathelicidin antimicrobial peptide (37 residues), a host-defence peptide with antimicrobial and wound-healing activity. No approved product; studied clinically in chronic venous leg ulcers. The registry note that 'FDA lacks sufficient safety information' is to be verified against FDA documents.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** membrane-active antimicrobial activity and immunomodulatory/angiogenic effects in wound models; human wound-healing signal in two randomised trials.

## Evidence summary

| Tier | Finding | Ref. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Venous leg ulcer healing RCT (2014) | [10.1111/wrr.12211](https://doi.org/10.1111/wrr.12211) |
| 1 | Multicentric RCT (2021) | [10.1111/wrr.12977](https://doi.org/10.1111/wrr.12977) |

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications.** Trial doses/formulations are study conditions.

## Important safety information and cautions

Local use in trials was well tolerated; systemic toxicity profile unestablished. Unregulated injectable supply adds risks.

## Contraindications

None established.

## Side effects

Trial data: transient local itching/pain; generally mild.

## Product quality

No approved product; market supply unregulated.

## Handling and storage

No guidance for unapproved supply.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A natural antimicrobial peptide being studied for wound healing. **What do we know?** Two randomised trials support leg-ulcer healing. **What don't we know?** Whether it becomes a medicine. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No approved product despite two positive RCTs
- Systemic safety unestablished
- FDA safety-information note to be verified and cited
