# Melanotan II

## Summary

Superpotent cyclic alpha-MSH analogue (Ac-Nle-Asp-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Lys lactam). Studied for tanning and erectile function in early human work; development was abandoned over safety and tolerability concerns (nausea, blood-pressure effects, and priapism reports). No approved product exists; it is sold illicitly for tanning.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** melanocortin-receptor agonism (MC1R-driven tanning; MC3R/MC4R effects including erectile activity in animal work and appetite effects).

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Any use is unapproved; study doses are not recommendations.

## Important safety information and cautions

Development was halted in part over safety signals: marked nausea, blood-pressure changes, and priapism reports; long-term melanocyte effects unstudied. Supply is illicit and unregulated — reported contaminants include the wrong peptide and unknown impurities.

## Contraindications

None formally established; unapproved in all jurisdictions. Precautionary: cardiovascular disease, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Nausea (dose-limiting), flushing, appetite suppression, hyperpigmentation of skin and naevi; priapism and blood-pressure changes reported.

## Product quality

No legitimate supply exists; seized samples have shown identity failures. Never a medicine.

## Handling and storage

Instructions deliberately not given.

## Regulatory and sport status

| Jurisdiction | Status |

## Notes for clinicians

If use is reported, document dose and source; consider blood-pressure monitoring and dermatological review of naevi; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** An abandoned experimental tanning drug sold illegally. **What do we know?** It tans skin but with severe nausea and other concerning effects. **What don't we know?** Long-term harm — which is why it was abandoned. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- Development abandoned; no phase 3 data
- No post-marketing surveillance (never marketed)
- Independent analyses of seized/market supply partially published; to be entered
