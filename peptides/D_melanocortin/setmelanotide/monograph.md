# Setmelanotide

## Summary

Melanocortin-4 receptor agonist with a disulfide-bridged cyclic octapeptide core (Ac-Arg-Cys-D-Ala-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Cys-NH2). Approved in the US (2020) and EU (2021) for obesity due to rare genetic deficiencies in the MC4R pathway (POMC, PCSK1, LEPR). Not approved for general obesity.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** MC4R agonism in the central melanocortin pathway, restoring satiety signalling that is deficient in the approved genetic disorders; reduces hunger and increases energy expenditure.

## Indications and dose

**Licensed:** obesity due to POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR deficiency (age ≥6; ≥2 in some regions); daily subcutaneous injection, titrated per label. Verify.

## Important safety information and cautions

Skin darkening and darkening of naevi (MC1R effects); spontaneous penile erections and sexual adverse effects (MC4R); depression and suicidal ideation — monitoring required; injection-site reactions.

## Contraindications

Per label; verify.

## Side effects

Injection-site reactions, hyperpigmentation, nausea, headache; sexual adverse effects in some patients.

## Notes for clinicians

Only the genetic indications are licensed; off-label use in common obesity is unproven. Monitor mood and skin changes; report adverse events.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A daily injection that restores a missing satiety signal in rare genetic obesity. **What do we know?** Meaningful weight loss in the licensed disorders. **What don't we know?** Benefit in common obesity; very long-term effects. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

## Evidence gaps

- No approval or strong data in common obesity
- Long-term safety beyond the rare-disease populations
