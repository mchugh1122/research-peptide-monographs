# KPV

## Summary

Tripeptide Lys-Pro-Val, the C-terminal fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH 11–13). Studied as an anti-inflammatory peptide in animal colitis models; no human trials and no approved product. Sold as a 'research' peptide, often alongside BPC-157 and TB-500.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (animal):** anti-inflammatory signalling via melanocortin pathways and PepT1-mediated intestinal uptake; human data absent.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications. No established dose.** Animal doses are study conditions only.

## Important safety information and cautions

No human data of any kind; injectable immunogenicity and supply-quality risks apply.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: pregnancy.
