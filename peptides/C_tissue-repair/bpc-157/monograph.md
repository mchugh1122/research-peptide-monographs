# BPC-157

## Summary

Synthetic 15-residue peptide, described as derived from a gastric protein; proposed in animal studies to aid tissue repair. Not approved anywhere, with very little human evidence.

## Mechanism of action

**Established:** none identified.

**Proposed (animal or in vitro only):** effects on tissue repair and blood-vessel signalling. Human pharmacokinetics not identified.

## Important safety information and cautions

FDA’s published safety summary for compounding raises possible immunogenicity for certain routes of administration, and difficulty characterising the active ingredient and peptide-related impurities. No safe or effective dose has been established.

## Contraindications

None established, because the compound has not been formally evaluated.

**Precautionary considerations (mechanism-inferred, unverified):** active cancer, given proposed effects on blood-vessel growth; pregnancy and breastfeeding, because there are no safety data.

## Side effects

Frequency not known. No human trial safety data identified. Injection-site and immune reactions are relevant given FDA’s immunogenicity concern. Animal-reported effects: to be added.

## Notes for clinicians

Useful questions: where it was obtained and in what form, the dose and duration used, other medicines and supplements, and the reason for use. Document the answers, consider the safety section above, and report any adverse event to the national scheme.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A lab-made chain of 15 amino acids.

**What do we know?** Mostly animal studies. It is not an approved medicine.

**What don’t we know?** Whether it works in people, what dose is safe, and what is actually in products sold online.

**What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you are using or considering it, where it came from, and how much you take.
