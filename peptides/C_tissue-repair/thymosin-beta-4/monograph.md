# Thymosin beta-4

## Summary

A 43-residue, N-terminally acetylated actin-sequestering peptide present in nearly all mammalian cells. Investigated for cardiac repair, wound healing and ocular surface disease; no approval has been granted. The 'research peptide' TB-500 is a fragment of it. Status: investigational.

## Mechanism of action

**Established (in vitro/animal):** G-actin sequestration regulating cytoskeletal dynamics; promotion of cell migration, survival, angiogenesis and reduced inflammation in injury models. The clinical translation of these effects is unproven.

## Indications and dose

**No licensed indications.** Phase 2 trials in cardiac and wound settings used doses per protocol; no established dose exists.

## Important safety information and cautions

Clinical trials have not established safety for any indication. Injectable immunogenicity risk applies; unregulated supply adds quality unknowns.

## Contraindications

None established. Precautionary: active malignancy, pregnancy.

## Side effects

Trial reports generally mild (injection-site reactions); comprehensive data unpublished.

## Product quality

No approved product; market supply (often as 'TB-500' or full-length material) is unregulated.

## Handling and storage

Instructions deliberately not given.

## Plain-language page

**What is it?** A natural cell protein studied for healing; never became a medicine. **What do we know?** Convincing animal repair biology; clinical proof lacking. **What don't we know?** Whether it works in patients. **What should I tell my doctor or pharmacist?** That you use it and where it came from.

- Long-term safety unknown
